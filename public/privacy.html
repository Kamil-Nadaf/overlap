Overlap privacy policy

Overlap is a free group scheduling tool at kamil-nadaf.github.io/overlap. Last updated 30 October 2026.

What Overlap stores

  • Event details: title, optional description, dates, time window, chosen meeting time and meeting link.
  • Participant details: the name a person types, the time slots they mark, an optional PIN (stored only as a salted hash), and an optional email address used for the meeting confirmation and reminders.
  • Email addresses are never shown to other participants and are only readable by the event's organizer and the reminder service.
  • Events and everything attached to them (names, availability, email addresses) are deleted automatically one day after the meeting ends, or 60 days after the last activity if no meeting was ever scheduled. The organizer can also delete an event at any time.

Feedback

If you use the Feedback button, Overlap stores your message, the optional name or email you typed, and the page you were on. It is used only to improve Overlap and to reply to you if you asked. Ask the contact address below to have a message removed.

Google sign-in (optional, organizers only)

When an organizer chooses "Send from my Gmail", Overlap asks Google for one permission: gmail.send (send email on your behalf).

  • It is used only to send the meeting confirmation email, with a calendar file, to the people who left an address on that event.
  • Overlap cannot read, list, search or delete anything in your mailbox.
  • The access token stays in your browser's memory for that one action. Overlap does not store it, and does not have a server that receives it.
  • Overlap's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

Overlap does not sell or share personal data, and has no ads or analytics. Data is stored with the hosting provider Supabase and the site is served by GitHub Pages. Reminder emails are sent through an email provider chosen by the site operator.

Your choices

Leaving an email address is optional. To have your data removed, ask the event organizer to delete the event, or contact kamilnadaf.phy@gmail.com.
